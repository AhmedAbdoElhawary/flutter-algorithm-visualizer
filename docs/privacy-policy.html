AlgoDive

Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Last updated September 2026 · Applies to the AlgoDive mobile app (com.elhawary.algodive)

AlgoDive is an algorithm learning app. This policy explains exactly what it stores, why, and how to get rid of it. It is written to be read, not to be skimmed past.

The short version

  • You can use almost the whole app without an account. Nothing leaves your phone in that mode.
  • Your code runs on your device. It is never uploaded to a server.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or run advertising trackers.
  • You can delete your account and all its data from inside the app at any time.

1. Who is responsible

AlgoDive is an independent, open-source project by Ahmed Abdo Elhawary. For any privacy question or request, contact elhawarydev@gmail.com.

2. Using AlgoDive without an account

You can browse the visualizers, open problems, write code and run it without signing in. In this mode your display name, your progress and your saved code are stored only in your device's local storage. They are not transmitted anywhere, and they are removed when you uninstall the app.

3. What we collect if you create an account

Creating an account is optional. It exists so your progress survives a lost phone and follows you across devices.

DataWhyWhere it is stored
Email address To identify your account, let you sign in, and send password resets Firebase Authentication
Password To secure your account Firebase Authentication — stored hashed. AlgoDive never sees or stores your password.
Display name Shown on your profile screen Firebase Authentication
Problem progress — which problems you solved, attempted or bookmarked, your submitted code, and timestamps To show your statistics, streak and history, and to sync them across your devices Cloud Firestore, under users/<your-user-id>/

4. Analytics and crash reports

Firebase Analytics

We collect anonymous, aggregated usage events — for example that a sorting visualizer was opened, or that a problem was submitted. This tells us which parts of the app are actually used so we know what to improve. These events are not linked to your email address and are not used for advertising.

Sentry (crash reporting)

When the app crashes, a crash report is sent to Sentry. It contains the error, the stack trace, and basic device information such as the operating system version and device model. Crash reporting runs in release builds only. It helps us fix bugs we cannot reproduce ourselves.

5. What we never collect

  • Your contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera.
  • Your precise location.
  • Advertising identifiers. AlgoDive contains no ad networks.

6. Who your data is shared with

We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not share it with advertisers. Your data is processed only by the service providers that make the app work:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Analytics) — Firebase privacy
  • Sentry (crash reporting) — Sentry privacy

We may disclose data if we are legally required to do so by a valid legal process.

7. How long data is kept

  • Account data and progress — kept until you delete your account, then removed as described below.
  • Crash reports — retained by Sentry for up to 90 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Analytics events — retained by Firebase Analytics under Google's standard retention period.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Access — your data is visible to you on the Profile screen in the app.
  • Correction — you can change your display name in the app at any time.
  • Deletion — you can delete your account and all associated data from Profile → Settings → Delete account, or by following the instructions on the account deletion page.
  • Use without an account — you can simply not sign in. The app remains usable.

If you are in the EEA or UK, the legal basis for processing your account data is performance of a contract (providing the sync service you asked for), and for analytics and crash reporting it is our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it.

9. Children

AlgoDive is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us at elhawarydev@gmail.com and we will delete it.

10. Security

Traffic between the app and Firebase is encrypted in transit. Firestore security rules restrict every document under users/<user-id>/ so that it can only be read or written by that signed-in account. No system is perfectly secure, but we do not store payment details or any sensitive category of personal data.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top of this page and note the change in the app's release notes. The current version is always published at this URL.

12. Contact

Questions, requests or complaints: elhawarydev@gmail.com
