THE RULES
Lines before boxes
A group is separated by space first, a hairline second, a filled card only when it is genuinely a distinct object.
No shadow, no blur
Depth is one grey step. Radius is 12-14px everywhere, 999px only on the pills that are truly pills.
Colour is information
If a colour is not carrying a difficulty, a state, or an action, it is grey.

Only indigo and a trace of cyan reach the background, at 28-36% opacity in one broad soft band rising from the bottom edge — enough to light the glass from below and no more. At label sizes all four fail contrast on black, which is why the primary action is white.

THE GLASS RECIPE — one material, three depths
Recessed
4% fill, blur 14, hairline 9%. Track backgrounds, chip rows, inactive segments.
Card — the default
5.5% fill, blur 22 + saturate 140%, hairline 13%, top inset highlight, 44px drop shadow.
Floating
10% fill, blur 30, hairline 20%. Live widget, tab bar, sheets, the solved moment.
Depth comes from fill and blur only — no coloured glows and no gradient borders. A card never sits on another card; if two glass layers must overlap, the upper one steps to the next depth.
