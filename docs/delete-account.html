Delete your AlgoDive account

Version 1.0 · Last updated September 2026 · Applies to the AlgoDive mobile app (com.elhawary.algodive)

You can delete your AlgoDive account and everything stored with it at any time. There are two ways to do it.

Option 1 — From inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open AlgoDive and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap the settings icon in the top-right corner.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Enter your password to confirm it is really you, then confirm the deletion.

Deletion happens immediately. You will be signed out and returned to the login screen.

Option 2 — By email

If you cannot access the app — for example you lost your device — send an email from the address your account is registered with to:

elhawarydev@gmail.com

Subject: AlgoDive account deletion request

The request must come from the registered email address so we can verify ownership. We will delete the account and confirm by reply within 30 days, and in practice much sooner.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Your email address and display namePermanently deleted from Firebase Authentication
Your passwordPermanently deleted with the account
Solved / attempted / bookmarked problemsPermanently deleted from Cloud Firestore
Your submitted code and practice historyPermanently deleted from Cloud Firestore
Local data on your deviceCleared as part of the same action

What is not deleted

Anonymous, aggregated analytics events and crash reports are not tied to your account and cannot be traced back to you individually, so they are not removed by an account deletion. Crash reports are deleted automatically within 90 days.

This cannot be undone

Deleting your account is permanent. Your streak, statistics and solved problems are gone and cannot be restored. If you only want to stop syncing, you can log out instead and keep using the app as a guest.

Retention after deletion

Once the deletion completes, no personal data is retained. Routine encrypted backups held by our infrastructure providers may keep residual copies for a short period before they expire on their normal rotation schedule.
